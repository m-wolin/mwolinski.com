## About Me

I’m a structural engineer and self-taught developer. This site is a place where I write about the intersection of engineering and code — structural analysis, automation, and the tools I build along the way. I hope you’ll enjoy exploring it and find something useful along the way.

Over the years, I’ve successfully developed both internal and open-source tools using the APIs of Autodesk Robot, Autodesk Revit, RFEM, and IDEA StatiCa. I’ve also built numerous internal tools for companies in Excel, often combining a user-friendly interface with a powerful backend to automate repetitive tasks and streamline engineering workflows.

If you have a question, an idea, or simply want to get in touch, don’t hesitate to reach out.

Feel free to visit my GitHub and LinkedIn profiles as well!
